III.

Acquisitions

We acquire fine audio.

It started in 1999, when Adam offered to sell a college-town hi-fi shop’s trade-ins on early eBay. That became StereoBuyers, and we’ve been buying ever since. Single components, complete systems, record collections and estates, handled with care and discretion.

  1. i

    Tell us what you have

    Maker, model and a few photographs. Paperwork and original packaging help but aren’t required.

  2. ii

    Receive a considered offer

    We explain how we arrived at the number. Prefer to trade toward something in the collection? We can do that too.

  3. iii

    We take it from there

    Bring it to the Navy Yard, or ask us about collection from your home. Larger systems and estates by arrangement.

The founder

“We don’t judge, we educate. What sounds good to you is what’s right for you.”

Adam grew up in New Jersey with the stereo on from morning to night, taking things apart to see how they worked. He learned the craft on the sales floors of Rabsons and Innovative Audio, founded StereoBuyers in 1999 and Resolution Audio Video in 2009. Some of the team have been beside him for fifteen years.
