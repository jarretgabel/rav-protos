We buy hi-fi.
Have since 1999.

It started in a college town hi-fi shop. Adam offered to sell their trade-ins on early eBay for a cut. That became StereoBuyers, and we’ve been buying ever since: single pieces, whole systems, record collections, estates.

Tell us what you’ve got. We’ll tell you what it’s worth and how we got there. If you’d rather trade toward something on the floor, we can do that too.

  • Pre-owned amplifiers, preamps, sources and speakers
