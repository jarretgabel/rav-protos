The Room

An afternoon,
yours alone.

The listening room sits in a century-old naval building at the Brooklyn Navy Yard: stone walls, timber beams, a deep leather couch. Visits are by appointment only, so while you’re here, the room is yours.

Bring the records you know by heart. We’ll play them on a few systems and listen alongside you. Adam and the team will guide you, but your ears decide. If you don’t hear the difference, don’t buy it.
