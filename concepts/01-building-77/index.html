  1. 1999

    An Ithaca College student offers to sell a local hi-fi shop’s used gear on early eBay. StereoBuyers is born.

  2. 2003

    Adam learns the craft on the sales floor at Innovative Audio in Manhattan, and meets his wife there.

  3. 2009

    Resolution Audio Video opens. Two back-to-back townhouse projects make brownstones the specialty.

  4. 2012

    Hurricane Sandy floods Red Hook. We rebuild.

  5. 2025

    A building fire in Red Hook takes it all again. We rebuild again, with a lot of help.

  6. Now

    Building 77, Brooklyn Navy Yard. A new listening room, open by appointment.
